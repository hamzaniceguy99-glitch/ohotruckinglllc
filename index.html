Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you make it there by tomorrow morning?

We will work it out against lawful driving hours and tell you honestly. A yes that depends on a driver running over hours is not a yes, and it is not worth the load.

Are you a broker?

No. We are a motor carrier with our own authority and our own equipment. We do not take loads and re-sell them, and we hold no broker authority or bond.

How much is my freight covered for?

Cargo insurance is carried to a stated limit per load and confirmed before dispatch. If your freight is worth more, tell us in advance so cover can be arranged rather than discovered missing after a claim.

Do you haul hazmat?

Not unless agreed in writing beforehand. Hazardous materials require endorsements and placarding, and we will not take that on casually.

What about detention?

Stated in the rate confirmation up front. Drivers sitting at a dock are burning hours they need for the run, so the terms are agreed before dispatch rather than argued afterwards.

Do you provide tracking?

Yes on expedited and dedicated work, and updates on request otherwise. Runs are electronically logged as the regulations require.

Got freight to move?

Send the commodity, weight and lanes for a written rate with a realistic transit time.
