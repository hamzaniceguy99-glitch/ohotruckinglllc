Four freight services, all quoted to transit times the hours of service rules allow.

Program 01 · Palletised and packaged

Dry van freight

Standard dry van work for shippers moving packaged goods. We confirm the weight and pallet count before dispatch because an overweight trailer is the shipper's problem and ours, and we would rather find the discrepancy at the dock than at a scale house.

Program 02 · Secured properly

Flatbed freight

Flatbed work lives or dies on securement. Straps, chains and tarping are done to the cargo securement standards rather than to whatever gets the truck moving, and we will tell a shipper when a load as presented cannot be legally secured.

Program 03 · Same truck, same week

Dedicated lanes

A shipper moving the same lane every week gets a better rate and the same driver, who learns the facility, the dock staff and the paperwork. Rates are agreed for the term rather than re-quoted against a spot market that moves every day.

Program 04 · Fast, and still legal

Expedited freight

Urgent freight is where the pressure to break hours is strongest. We calculate what is achievable within the hours of service rules, offer a team if the distance genuinely needs one, and decline when the requested time is not legally possible rather than accepting and hoping.
